# walldrift

Random popular wallpapers from online sources, filtered by topics you choose.

walldrift keeps a few images downloaded ahead, so changing the wallpaper is instant and still
works offline. Favorite the ones you like to keep a copy, or ban the ones you don't to never see
them again.

## Use

- **Left-click** the panel icon for the menu: the current image's credit (click it to open its
  page), Next wallpaper, Save to favorites, and Never show this again.
- **Middle-click** the icon for the next wallpaper.
- The wallpaper also changes on a timer, at login, and (optionally) when you unlock the screen.

## Settings

Right-click the icon > Configure:

- **General:** how often to change, topics (for example `nature, space, minimal`, or empty for the
  most popular images overall), minimum resolution (by default your largest monitor's), the
  favorites folder, and how much to cache.
- **Sources:** turn sources on or off, how often each is picked, per-source topics, and
  source-specific filters.

## Turn off other wallpaper changers

Cinnamon's background slideshow and wallpaper extensions such as cinnamon-dynamic-wallpaper
overwrite the wallpaper walldrift sets. walldrift tells you when it finds one, with a button to
the settings page that turns it off.

## Requirements

Cinnamon 6.0 or later with Python 3.11 or later, as on Linux Mint 22 and LMDE 6. Nothing else
needs installing.
